## Phase 1 — B3 analytic reference posterior (`figure_b3_reference_posterior.pdf`)
Reference `p(S_I,σ|G) ∝ L(mDI=S_I·σ)·prior` by grid forward-sim (BDF). **Numerically
confirms the B2 non-identifiability proof**: the likelihood ridge lies on the fiber.

Aggregate over 8 observations (mDI percentiles 20–80):
| metric | mean ± std |
|---|---|
| reference MAP mDI rel-err | **2.4% ± 2.0%** |
| reference across-fiber CV (mDI) | **10.0% ± 1.4%** (tight ⇒ mDI identifiable) |
| π* vs ref sliced-W2 | 0.203 ± 0.142 |
| π* mass in ref-HPD95 | 18.8% ± 2.6% |
| π* across-fiber W1(log mDI) | 0.489 ± 0.011 (consistent offset) |
| π* mDI CV | 80.5% ± 3.5% (over-dispersed vs ref 10%) |

Reading: reference is a thin crescent on the true fiber; the canonical A-DCVAE π*
follows the fiber **direction** but is over-dispersed with a consistent across-fiber
offset — an honest, reproducible approximation gap (ε_app/ε_inc made visible).

## Phase 2 — B7 three-guarantee ablation (`figure_b7_ablation.pdf`)
Data/split fixed (seed 42); only training randomness varies over 5 seeds. Three
training/inference noise sources ablated in isolation (DISCUSSION §E taxonomy). Run
**twice**: first at 10k samples/500-epoch cap (exploratory, fast), then reproduced at
**canonical scale** (50k samples/10000-epoch cap/patience=200 — matching the user's
original `iter_cvae` checkpoint settings) to confirm the signal isn't a small-scale
artifact.

| variant | κ (10k → 50k canonical) | mDI rel-err (10k → 50k) | along-fiber std stoch/det (10k → 50k) | ε_inc (50k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| full | 0.394±0.01 → **0.406±0.00** | 0.070±0.07 → **0.019±0.00** | 0.915/0.000 → 0.718/**0.000** | 0.029±0.00 |
| N1 off (no condition) | 0.616±0.05 → **0.709±0.04** | 0.162±0.07 → 0.126±0.02 | 1.957/0.494 → 2.332/**1.715** | 0.036±0.00 |
| N2 off (no target) | 0.385±0.01 → **0.404±0.01** | 0.039±0.02 → 0.019±0.01 | 0.907/0.000 → 0.779/**0.000** | 0.027±0.00 |

All 15 canonical runs completed (~17.2h wall clock, 2026-07-13 23:07 → 2026-07-14 16:20,
unattended). Findings **reproduce at canonical scale, with two refinements**:
- **N1 (condition noise) → Thm 1 stability**: κ 0.41→0.71 (even larger gap at 50k than at
  10k) toward the κ=1 limit. Divergence stays 0 (bounded/clamped system — effect is on
  *rate* κ, not blow-up). ✅
- **N4 (injection noise) → Thm 3 non-collapse**: deterministic ping-pong collapses
  along-fiber spread to 0 for `full`/`no_target`, confirmed at canonical scale. But for
  `no_condition`, the **deterministic** spread is no longer ~0 (0.49 at 10k → **1.72 at
  50k**) — a richer illustration: when κ is far from 0 (weak contraction), even the
  *deterministic* mean-map ping-pong fails to fully collapse to a point within the fixed
  step budget, reinforcing that N1/κ and N4/injection-noise are doing distinct jobs. ✅
- **N2 (target noise) → Thm 2 direction**: still *no measurable isolated benefit*
  (mDI err ≈ full at both scales). Consistent with DISCUSSION B12's own view that Thm 2
  is a background principle, not load-bearing.
- **ε_inc ≈ 0.03** across all variants at both scales: π* is near self-consistent.

## Phase 3 — B4 strong baselines (`figure_b4_baselines.pdf`)
All three methods trained on an identical context (same data/split/normalizer) per run.
Run at **10k** (exploratory) and **50k canonical** scale; the two scales gave
**qualitatively different conclusions**, traced to a training-budget confound (below).

### 10k-scale (5 seeds × 5 ref = 25 runs/method)
| method | ref-HPD95 cov ↑ | along-fiber std ↑ | sliced-W2 ↓ | mDI err ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-DCVAE (dual, ours) | 0.204 ± 0.02 | 0.882 ± 0.03 | 0.156 ± 0.06 | 0.022 ± 0.02 |
| NPE-flow (single RealNVP) | **0.814 ± 0.03** | 1.232 ± 0.13 | **0.148 ± 0.07** | 0.044 ± 0.03 |
| det-reg (point) | 1.000* | **0.000** | 0.291 ± 0.06 | 0.033 ± 0.03 |

### 50k canonical scale, v1 — ⚠️ confounded, superseded
| method | ref-HPD95 cov ↑ | along-fiber std ↑ | sliced-W2 ↓ | mDI err ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-DCVAE (dual, ours) | 0.203 ± 0.02 | 0.712 ± 0.01 | **0.170** ± 0.10 | **0.016** ± 0.01 |
| NPE-flow | 0.610 ± 0.07 | 1.995 ± 0.22 | 0.481 ± 0.07 (3× worse) | 0.012 ± 0.01 |
| det-reg (point) | 1.000* | 0.000 | 0.284 ± 0.03 | 0.040 ± 0.05 |

At v1 canonical scale the ranking **flipped** (A-DCVAE now wins sliced-W2 and is
competitive on direction), the opposite of the 10k finding. **This flip is confounded**:
`train_npe_flow`/`train_det_regressor` had **early-stopping patience hardcoded to 40**,
while A-DCVAE (via `B7.train_variant`) used the config default of 200 — a 5× budget
mismatch that was present in *both* the 10k and 50k runs, but may bite harder at 50k
(same epoch-patience represents proportionally less exploration when each epoch covers
5× more batches). Fixed 2026-07-14: `b4_baselines.py` now takes a uniform `--patience`
CLI flag applied to all three methods; **v2 rerun (patience=200 for everyone) launched at
canonical scale, in progress** — this results file will be updated once it completes.

Implication for the paper (W4/B11 honesty) **pending v2**: do not yet write "decoupling
beats NPE" or "decoupling doesn't beat NPE" as a settled claim — the only settled part is
that A-DCVAE recovers structure (non-zero along-fiber spread, direction accuracy) while
det-reg collapses to an uninformative point. The NPE comparison awaits the fair rerun.

> ⚠️ Earlier bug (now fixed): the very first 50k attempt applied a 10k eval normalizer to

> by training all methods on the identical context each run.
